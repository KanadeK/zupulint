# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and patient. Welcome people across backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Genealogy can involve contested identities and living relatives, so treat uncertainty and cultural conventions with care.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, impersonation, publishing another person's private information, or uploading real genealogy data without authorization.

## Privacy is part of conduct

Issues, pull requests, logs, screenshots, and fixtures must use fictional or thoroughly sanitized data. If sensitive content is posted accidentally, stop redistributing it and notify the maintainer through private vulnerability reporting.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, restrict participation, or report serious abuse to the hosting provider. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's Security reporting channel. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as practical and avoid conflicts of interest when reviewing a report.
